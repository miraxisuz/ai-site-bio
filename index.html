07 Thoughts

Development2026

Why animations are not just decoration

Good motion design isn't cosmetic — it's communication. The way a UI moves tells users what to expect next, builds trust, and makes interfaces feel human.

Mindset2026

What boxing taught me about engineering

Discipline. Recovery. The discipline to train when you don't feel like it maps exactly to the discipline to debug at midnight. Winning is a habit, not an event.
